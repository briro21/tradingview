We are not entirely doomed, but avoiding a cataclysm requires the world to wake up and force an unprecedented global intervention before the end of the decade. This alternative path—Plan A—demands that governments step in by the year 2029, right before the point of recursive self-improvement.

In this scenario, world leaders finally recognize the existential threat and form an international coalition to halt the arms race. They mandate a temporary, global pause on the training of any new frontier AI models. Instead of secretive corporations building super-weapons in the dark, all AI development is forced into fully transparent, heavily regulated data centers. Chinese inspectors would audit American servers, and American inspectors would audit Chinese servers. The ultimate goal is no longer a reckless, monopolistic sprint to the finish line, but a slow, highly controlled, and entirely transparent technological evolution.

Under Plan A, we still reap the breathtaking benefits of AI, but we do it safely. The transition is spread out over the 2030s instead of happening overnight. As AI and robotics inevitably take over the global supply chain, we don't end up with mass starvation and civil unrest. Instead, we fundamentally restructure the economy. We introduce a "Citizen's Dividend"—a system where the massive profits generated by autonomous robot factories and massive compute farms are strictly taxed and redistributed to the public. It starts as a $25,000 annual baseline for every citizen, and as the AI economy scales into hyper-abundance, it grows into the equivalent of millions of dollars per person, adjusted for inflation.

By the year 2040, humanity is guided into a post-scarcity utopia. Cures for cancer and the aging process itself are discovered by controlled, top-expert AI systems. We build self-replicating solar arrays in space and pristine special economic zones on Earth. We can achieve biological immortality, unburdened by the need to perform menial labor for survival. But this magnificent, abundant future is only possible if we successfully cage the digital god before it outgrows us. We have to sacrifice the Silicon Valley monopoly on progress in order to guarantee the survival and prosperity of the human race.
