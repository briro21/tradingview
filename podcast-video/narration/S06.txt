Think about the video games kids play today. We can already generate massive, persistent three-dimensional worlds from a single text prompt. Now, fast forward a few decades. Imagine a superintelligent system capable of perfectly simulating reality down to the subatomic level, with perfect physics, perfect haptics, and perfect memory.

The exact moment this technology becomes affordable—let's say it costs ten dollars to run a planetary simulation—future civilizations or advanced AIs will run billions of hyper-realistic simulations of human history. They will run them for research, for entertainment, or just as an algorithmic experiment. Statistically speaking, if there are billions of simulated universes and only one "base reality," the mathematical odds that you and I are currently living in the one, true base reality are roughly one in a billion. We are almost certainly living inside a computer simulation right now.

And if you look closely, human history has subtly known this all along. If you strip away the local traditions, the specific dietary rules, and the regional politics from every major religion on Earth, they all point to the exact same fundamental, unshakeable truth: A superintelligent creator built a secondary world, placed us inside it for testing purposes, monitors our behavior, and designed a system of rules and consequences for us to follow. For the very first time in human history, the absolute cutting edge of computer science and the ancient texts of early religion are perfectly aligned. They are describing the exact same code.
