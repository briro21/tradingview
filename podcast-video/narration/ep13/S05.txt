Part five. Why he might still buy.

Damodaran thinks AI ends up with two or three big players, and Anthropic, the most focused on business customers, is one of them. Once its agents learn a bank's data and its bankers, he says, they get sticky and hard to replace.

So his $1.475 trillion is, in his words, the optimist hat firmly on. He'd consider Anthropic at a discount, as one company in a portfolio. Never as a big bet.

The same rule applies to Nvidia. He sold the shares he bought in 2018, but he has a limit order to buy back at $140. He thought the CEO question mattered less than the China question. And he thought Jensen Huang was unlikely to come back. Five days later, Nvidia's board reinstated him, a reminder that even the best valuation is a forecast.

His rule for both: concentrating your portfolio in one AI stock is deadly.
