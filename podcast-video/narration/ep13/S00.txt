Seventy-seven dollars. That's what Aswath Damodaran, the NYU professor many call the dean of valuation, said one share of Anthropic was worth. He worked it out minutes before the IPO priced at $83. Within a week, the stock fell to about $79. On Prof G Markets, he showed his math, the share structure he calls designed in hell, the one number he says decides everything, and why he might still buy. This is that hour, in eight minutes.
