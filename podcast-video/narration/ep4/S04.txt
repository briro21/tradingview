Now the pendulum swings back. The destruction stops, and creation begins. Stone by stone, the crumbling corners of the facade are realigned, and fresh mortar seals the wounds. Up top, the skeletal roofline is crowned again with solid timber and heavy, dark slate. For decades, the silhouette was jagged against the horizon. Suddenly, it is whole.

Inside, the grand staircase, once a hazard of rotting wood, is rebuilt in rich, dark mahogany. Fresh plaster glides over the exposed brick, erasing the water stains like a deep wound healing in fast forward. Even the air changes. The damp smell of decay gives way to cut wood, fresh paint, and raw potential. The house is waking up.
