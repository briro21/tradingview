A structure becomes a home when it learns to play with light. For years, the windows were black voids, empty eyes staring into the wilderness. Now pristine glass is fitted back into the ornate frames, and when the morning sun hits them, the whole mansion changes. Sunbeams cut across new parquet floors, laid in perfect geometric patterns.

The colors of a forgotten era bleed back in. Deep emerald returns to the library walls. Chipped crown moldings are restored to brilliant white, pulling your eye up to the towering ceilings. Crystal chandeliers scatter the light into a thousand tiny prisms. This is the phase of obsession. Carved rosettes above the doorways. Heavy brass hinges on oak doors. The details that turn construction into art.
