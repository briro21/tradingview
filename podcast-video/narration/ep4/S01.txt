Time is a quiet thief. It doesn't steal a legacy all at once. It erodes it, season by season. First the laughter from the grand halls, then the warmth from the hearth, and finally the roof that was built to protect those memories. Look at this place. It is not just an abandoned mansion. It is a monument to impermanence. Ivy has crawled up the stone facade, claiming it back for the earth. Nature is patient. It waits for us to leave, and then it quietly erases our footsteps.

The glass of the grand conservatory lies shattered in the dust like crushed diamonds. Floorboards once polished to a mirror finish are buckled and warped by decades of rain. The grand staircase curves upward into nothing but collapsed ceilings and empty doorways. And yet, beneath the wind howling through the broken rafters, the bones of this house are still strong. It is not waiting for a miracle. It is waiting for an intervention. So let's rewind the clock.
