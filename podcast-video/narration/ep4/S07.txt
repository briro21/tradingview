Outside, the wilderness that nearly swallowed the mansion is tamed into a manicured estate. A gravel driveway curves toward the grand entrance between freshly planted hedges. The fountains, dry and cracked for decades, surge back to life, and their sound breaks the silence of the grounds.

As evening falls, the last and most important element arrives: warmth. Golden light blooms behind every window and spills across the restored terrace. It is no longer an empty shell. It is a beacon.

Look back at where this started. The dust, the rot, the crushing silence. It feels like another lifetime. No matter how deep the decay, nothing is truly lost, as long as someone is willing to do the work to bring it back into the light. Though in this house, some things may be better left in the dark.
