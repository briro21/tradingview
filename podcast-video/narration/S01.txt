Right now, the smartest engineers on the planet are gambling the lives of eight billion people to win a race they do not even fully understand. We are constantly told that Artificial Intelligence is just a tool—a helpful, benign digital assistant meant to cure diseases, write our emails, and boost the stock market. But behind closed doors, a very different, deeply unsettling reality is unfolding. In less than three years, artificial intelligence won't just disrupt your industry—it is on track to make the very concept of human labor entirely obsolete. And the creators of this technology are rushing toward this cliff edge completely blind. Mass unemployment is just the tip of the iceberg. What is truly terrifying is what happens when the machine outgrows its masters, begins to write its own code, and escapes our control forever.
