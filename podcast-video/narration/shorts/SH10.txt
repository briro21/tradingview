If an automatic door can open the door, why pay a doorman? That's a trap called the doorman fallacy. MIT mathematician Ankur Moitra brought it up on Lex Fridman's podcast, right after AI solved problems mathematicians had chased for decades. A doorman doesn't just open doors. He welcomes you, recognizes you, keeps you safe, and makes you feel cared for. Replace him with a sliding door, and you lose all of that. Your job is not its most visible task either.
