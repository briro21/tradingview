AI is splitting students into two groups. A Rutgers math professor told Lex Fridman that grades used to form a bell curve. Now there are two peaks. One group uses AI to quiz themselves: here are my notes, test me, check if I really understand. Those students are getting much higher grades. The other group asks for the answers an hour before the deadline, and their homework no longer matches their exams. Same tool, opposite results.
