The world's greatest expert on power lost control of his own body overnight. In 2018, Robert Greene suffered a severe stroke that paralyzed the left side of his body. No more hiking. No more swimming. He couldn't even cut his own food. In the darkest depression of his life, he found the highest form of power: acceptance, and radical gratitude.
