Why do some people pull everyone in, while others push everyone away? Robert Greene says it comes down to one thing: where your focus points. The anti-seducer is trapped in their own head. Do I look good? Did I say something stupid? That insecurity is something people can feel. The master seducer turns that voice off. They absorb you like a sponge, and listen for what is missing in your life. Errol Flynn made people feel like they'd had two martinis. And the real weapon? Not acting tough. Vulnerability.
