A man who has advised every White House since nine eleven says we are entering the eight most dangerous weeks of our lives. Three crises are colliding at once: a tense transfer of power in America, a three day bombing plan for Iran, and a dark winter for Ukraine. Professor Robert Pape laid it all out on The Diary of a CEO. This is that ninety minute conversation in ten minutes. How the chain could lead to world war three, and the three firebreaks he says can still stop it.
