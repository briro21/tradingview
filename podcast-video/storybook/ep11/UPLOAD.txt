- Anthropic's $895 billion of commitments vs. its slowing growth

- Why AMD and Broadcom can't simply replace Jensen Huang
- The neoclouds on Zitron's death watch
- Who pays: gamers, data center towns and the economy (with Steve from Gamers Nexus, who pushes back on blaming one man)
