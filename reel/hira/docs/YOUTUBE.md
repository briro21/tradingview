## Deskripsi

```
Muhammad came home from Cave Hira shaking, certain he was losing his mind. His wife Khadijah did not panic. She said one sentence that history still remembers: "Never! By God, God will never disgrace you."

This is the story of the night of the first revelation, told as a short animated film: the squeezing in the cave, the first five verses, the terrified run home, Khadijah's answer, and the warning from her cousin Waraqah bin Naufal that no one expected. No faces are shown; the angel is shown only as light.

Based on the narration of Aisha in Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 3 (the beginning of revelation). Visuals are a dramatization. The narration uses a synthetic voice.
```
